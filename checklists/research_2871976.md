## Companies House Financials (Verified)

**The single largest gap in this file.** Leaflet Books Ltd has filed "Total Exemption" accounts under the small-company / micro-entity exemption since the first full period. That means:

- **No profit & loss account is public.** Turnover, gross profit, wages, rent expense, net profit — none of it is on the Companies House record.
- **Balance sheets are filed but only in abbreviated form.** They are available on the Companies House website as PDFs (AA filings), but no third-party aggregator (Endole / OpenGovUK) exposes summary financials, which strongly suggests only statutory minimums are disclosed.
- **Continuity pattern is positive.** Confirmation statements and accounts have been filed on time every year from 2007 through 2025. No strike-off gazettes, no CCJs, no mortgages. That rules out the kind of distress flags present at the Gilded Acorn sample.
- **However, accounts are now overdue.** The period to 31 March 2025 should have been filed by 31 March 2026 but has not been. On a 30+ year track record of timely filing this is a departure and could be consistent with the owners winding down.
- **Bulk historical filing in 2007.** On 29 March 2007 the company filed total-exemption accounts for 1994–2005 in a single batch. That is unusual — it indicates historical non-compliance that was regularised at the name change in 2007 — but it is ancient history and no strike-off action ever succeeded.

**Inference (not verification):** A company with £2 of share capital, two directors, no charges, no employees shown on public aggregator summaries, no audit, and consistent "total exemption" filings since 1994 is a classic small-trader structure — partnership-like economics wrapped in a limited company for liability protection. The fact that the shop clearly trades (website, social media, reviews, PBFA membership, Bookshop.org activity, 50,000 titles) means the company is operational, not dormant.
