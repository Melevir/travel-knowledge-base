- Cumulative losses of £87,240 over ~2.5 years of operation

**Red flags found:**

### 3. Audit the Lease

**What the listing states:**

**What we know:**

- £12,000/year for 600 sq ft in WC2A is approximately £20/sq ft — significantly below market rate (market: £50–100/sq ft for this area)
- This below-market rent is the single most valuable asset in this deal
- The lease is charged to profit or loss on a straight line basis (per accounting policy)

**Missing — must request:**

- [ ] Lease start date and exact remaining term
- [ ] Assignment provisions — can the lease be assigned? What conditions? Does the landlord (LSE) have to consent?
- [ ] Whether the lease is inside or outside the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 (security of tenure)
- [ ] Rent review clauses — is £12K fixed for the full term or subject to review?
- [ ] Break clauses — can either party terminate early?
- [ ] Dilapidations schedule and obligations
- [ ] Keep-open clause (does the tenant have to keep trading?)
- [ ] Permitted use clause — is it restricted to bookshop/retail, or broader Class E?
- [ ] Service charges (if any) on top of the £12K rent
- [ ] Confirmation from the landlord that they will consent to assignment

### 13. Calculate Total Acquisition Cost

**What the listing states:**
- Asking price: £50K–£100K (includes fixtures £18K + stock £25K)
- Implied goodwill at midpoint: £75K - £43K tangibles = £32K

**What the accounts reveal:**
- Tangible fixed assets (net book value): £19,345 (close to the £18K listed for fixtures)
- Stock: £16,767 (NOT £25K as listed)
- The business has net liabilities of £87K

**Critical question: Is this an asset purchase or a share purchase?**
- If **share purchase**: you inherit ALL liabilities — £143K of debts, £46K of bank loans, negative equity of £87K. The asking price of £50–100K ON TOP of inheriting £143K of liabilities makes the effective price £193–243K for a loss-making business
- If **asset purchase**: you buy specific assets (stock, fixtures, lease, brand) and avoid the liabilities. But the bank loan is secured by the director's personal guarantee, not the company's assets — so it may not transfer. The "other creditors" of ~£97K remain unexplained

**Missing — must request:**
- [ ] Is this being sold as an asset purchase or share purchase?
- [ ] If share purchase: full list of all liabilities that transfer
- [ ] If asset purchase: exact list of assets included
- [ ] Will the bank loan (£45,853) be settled by the seller before completion?
- [ ] Will director loans (if any) be written off before completion?
- [ ] What happens to the "other creditors" totalling ~£97K?
